About

Engineering across design, coordination and management

I’m a Civil Engineer based in Colombia, with a Bachelor’s degree from Universidad Nacional de Colombia and a Master’s in Civil Engineering with emphasis on Construction Management from Universidad de Los Andes.

My career started in hydraulic engineering, planning and leading plumbing, wastewater and fire protection designs for buildings, infrastructure and airports, coordinated through BIM. I then supported the planning consultant office of Bogota’s Instituto de Desarrollo Urbano (IDU) with financial and investment monitoring of road infrastructure and public space works.

Since 2023 I’ve been reviewing commercial solar designs at Exactus Energy for buildings in the United States, Canada and Puerto Rico. I work in English, Spanish and French, with basic Japanese and Italian.
